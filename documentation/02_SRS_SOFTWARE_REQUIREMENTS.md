### Quick Summary of Specifications
- **Project Name:** CampusFix – Campus Maintenance and IT Fault Reporting & Management System
- **Actors:** Student/Reporter, Field Technician, System Administrator, Notification Engine
- **Core Modules:** Authentication & RBAC, Fault Reporting, Ticket Tracking & Lifecycle, Technician Fieldwork, Admin Triage & Analytics, Multi-Channel Notifications (In-App, SMTP Email, Mock SMS).
- **Security:** Bcrypt, PDO Prepared Statements, CSRF tokens, XSS output encoding, secure file upload validators.
